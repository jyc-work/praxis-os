# Philosophy

> **What PraxisOS believes about people, ideas and decisions.**

## The problem

Classic personal systems manage *things*: goals, projects, tasks, habits, notes,
journals. They answer:

> "What do I need to manage?"

PraxisOS starts one level higher. It asks:

> "**Why do I choose this?**"

and

> "**What kind of life is worth building — for me?**"

Between a person's values and their behaviour there is usually a fracture:
books are read and forgotten, lessons are learned and repeated, goals are set
and drift with mood. PraxisOS is an attempt to weld that fracture with
**engineering discipline**: stable identifiers, explicit states, machine-checkable
files, and a history that cannot quietly rewrite itself.

## The loop

```text
Reality
   ↓
Observation
   ↓
Reflection
   ↓
World Model
   ↓
Principle
   ↓
Decision
   ↓
Action / Experiment
   ↓
Outcome
   ↓
Review
   ↓
Update Principle
   └──────────────────→
```

The loop only closes when **Reality** is allowed to correct **Principle**.
Everything else in this system exists to make that correction legible.

## Five beliefs

### 1. The user owns the final judgment

AI agents working in PraxisOS are analysts, devil's advocates, retrievers,
structuring assistants and review partners. They are **not** life coaches,
moral judges, or decision makers. Every significant decision file must carry:

```text
Final Judgment: Human
```

### 2. Reality outranks ideology

No philosophy is accepted because of its authority. Every principle must be
open to being *falsified, revised, downgraded, or retired*. A system where
principles are never revised is a confirmation-bias engine wearing a journal's
clothes.

### 3. Principle outranks quote

PraxisOS is not a quotation library. What matters is the principle **the user
thinks through and owns**. A thinker's words enter only as `Source / Inspiration`
— never as the system's conclusion.

### 4. Action outranks consumption

Reading, bookmarking, watching and annotating are not completion. The ideal
path is:

```text
Input → Reflection → Principle Candidate → Action Rule → Experiment → Outcome
```

Any idea that has not been tested against reality defaults to:

```text
UNVALIDATED
```

### 5. Framework is public; data is private

The schema, templates, skills, prompts and methodology are open source. The
user's journals, assets, identity and relationships are private by construction
(`/private` is gitignored; the validator scans for leaks).

## What success is not

Success is not GitHub stars, downloads, token counts, or the number of notes.
Success metrics are behavioural:

- Can the user find, in five minutes, **why they did what they did**?
- Can a major decision be traced: **Value → Principle → Decision**?
- Does every significant decision have a review?
- Has the user ever revised a principle because reality said so?
- Do repeated mistakes decline over time?

## Final design principle

> **思想不是用来崇拜的，而是用来检验的。**
> Thought is not for worship, but for testing.
>
> **价值观不是写在纸上的，而是通过长期选择体现的。**
> Values are not written down; they are revealed by long-term choices.
>
> **原则不是永恒真理，而是当前最可靠的行为假设。**
> Principles are not eternal truths; they are the most reliable behavioural
> hypotheses currently available.
>
> **决策不是为了保证正确，而是为了在不确定环境中提高判断质量。**
> Decisions exist not to be right, but to raise the quality of judgment under
> uncertainty.
>
> **复盘不是为了后悔，而是为了修正模型。**
> Review exists not for regret, but for model correction.
>
> **最终目标不是构建完美系统，而是形成一个越来越独立、清醒、有行动能力的人。**
> The goal is not a perfect system; it is a person who is progressively more
> independent, clear-headed and capable of action.
